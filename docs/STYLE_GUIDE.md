# Kumarajiva-like style guide (project-internal)

## Target feel

Close to the readability of 罗什’s *Diamond* / *Vimalakīrti* Chinese: short cola, balanced cadence, less Indic repetition—while the **content** stays early Āgama.

## Transformation patterns

| Guṇabhadra-ish | Prefer |
|----------------|--------|
| 当观色无常。如是观者，则为正观。正观者，则生厌离；厌离者，喜贪尽；喜贪尽者，说心解脱。 | 谛观诸色，皆归无常。如是观者，名为正观。既生厌离，贪爱永灭，说心解脱。 |
| 欲令如是、不令如是 | 得大自在 / 不得自在 |
| 如实知 | 如实知 / 明见 |

## Keep

- Stock awakening formula: 我生已尽，梵行已立，所作已作，自知不受后有
- Place names in established Chinese Buddhist forms: 舍卫国祇树给孤独园

## Reject

See `glossary/forbidden_mahayana.txt`. Also reject modern slang and self-help tone in the literary column.
